# Hackathon submission draft — HyeTraq

**Track:** Build with buy (Agents on Open Rails)

**Name:** HyeTraq

**Tagline:** Research. Execute. Verify.

**One-line description:** HyeTraq is an evidence-first Web3 research agent designed to use paid data, browser, or inference services only when they add value, stay within a user-approved budget, and preserve the evidence behind both its findings and its spending.

**Problem:** Web3 research is fragmented across on-chain data, dashboards, APIs, and reports. Researchers spend time stitching together sources and may not know which data services were used or what an automated workflow cost.

**Proposed solution:** HyeTraq plans research from a specific question, prefers free bounded sources where possible, and is designed to purchase additional evidence through buy only after checking a live quote against a user-approved cap. Its final report should link sources and separate sourced facts from analysis and unknowns. Its spend ledger should include only verified payment evidence.
